# IKEA Dinner

## info
* About 1 hour
* 4 servings

## ingredients
### Meatballs
* 1 lb ground pork (or half and half pork/veal)
* 1/4 cup milk
* 1/4 cup seltzer water
* 1/4 cup grated onion
* 1 large egg
* 1/4 cup flour
* 1/4 cup bread crumbs
* Salt and pepper to taste
### Gravy
* 1/2 cup stock (chicken or beef)
* 2 tbsp flour


## steps
### Meatballs
1. Mix pork with wet ingredients until combined. Mix in flour, bread crumbs, salt and pepper. The mixture should be quite wet, but not dripping.
2. Form the meatballs, about 2tbsp (the size of a small egg). 
3. Heat a large pan at medium-high. Grease with vegetable oil or margarine.
4. In a single layer, place the meatballs on the pan. Lightly press down with a spatula to flatten. Cook until the outsides are dark brown, and the insides are cooked through (about 10 minutes per side).
### Gravy
5. Once meatballs are cooked, remove from pan. Add some stock or water to pan and scrape clean. Once bubbling, add flour and reduce heat. Mix until a thick paste forms.
6. Add more stock and cook until the gravy reaches a desired consistency.
7. Serve with buttered peas and mashed potatoes.
