# Pizza Dough

## info  
* 1h30 prep, overnight rise
* ~2-3 pizzas

## ingredients
* 1/4 tsp yeast (2g)
* 1 1/3 cups warm water (350g)
* 2 1/2 tsp salt (14g)
* 4 cups (500g) flour

## steps  
1. Measure out warm water into a large mixing bowl. Water should be warm enough that it's barely comfortable to touch. Dissolve salt in water before adding yeast, then let the yeast dissolve and activate before mixing in.
2. Add flour and mix with a wooden spoon until a shaggy dough forms.
3. Wet hands as necessary to prevent sticking, and fold dough until it forms a single large ball.
4. Flour a clean surface and knead dough, about 2 minutes. Return to oiled mixing bowl, cover and let rise overnight.
5. Remove from bowl and transfer to a floured surface. Being careful not to overwork dough, split into even parts and shape into balls. Make into pizzas or freeze for later
