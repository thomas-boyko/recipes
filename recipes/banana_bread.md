# Banana Bread

## info
* Servings: 1 loaf
* About 1 hour 15 minutes

## ingredients
*   2 cups all-purpose flour
*   1 tbsp. baking soda
*   1/4 tsp. salt
*   1/2 cup butter, melted
*   3/4 cup brown sugar
*   2 eggs, beaten
*   2 1/3 cups mashed overripe bananas (3-4 bananas)
* 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

## images
![Banana Bread](images/bananabread.jpg)

## steps
* Preheat oven to 350F and prepare a bread pan with cooking spray or parchment paper.
* In a large bowl, mix together melted butter and brown sugar until smooth. Stir in beaten eggs and mashed bananas until well blended.
* Add flour, baking soda, and salt and mix just until combined. Add chocolate chips and fold in.
* Pour batter into the pan.
* Bake for 60–65 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean (save for melted chocolate).
* Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
