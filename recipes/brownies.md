# Brownies

## info  
* About 45 minutes
* 1 8x8 inch pan, about 16 brownies

## ingredients
* 140g unsalted butter
* 250g granulated (white) sugar
* 75g cocoa powder
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 1 tsp vanilla extract 
* 2 large eggs
* 85g all-purpose flour
* 50g chocolate chips (OPTIONAL)

## steps  
*  Preheat oven to 325F, and line an 8x8 inch baking pan with parchment paper. Lightly greasse the lining.
* Melt butter in the microwave, stirring in 30-second increments to prevent burning.
* Add sugar, cocoa, and salt to butter in a large mixing bowl until combined.
* Add vanilla and eggs and mix until combined.
* Mix in flour carefully until combined. Careful not to overmix. If using additional chocolate, add it here.
* Spread batter in prepeared pan and bake for 20-25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean from the middle. Brownies should look a little underdone. They will turn out well if this is the case.
