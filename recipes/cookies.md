# Chocolate Chip Cookies

## info  
* About 45 minutes
* ~2 dozen

## ingredients
* 1 cup softened butter
* 1 cup brown sugar
* 1/2 cup granulated white sugar
* 2 eggs
* 1 tsp vanilla extract 
* 1/2 tsp salt
* 1 tsp baking soda
* 2 cups flour
* 2 cups semi sweet chocolate chips

## steps  
* Preheat oven to 350F prepare a baking sheet with cooking spray or parchment paper.
* Cream together butter and sugar in a large mixing bowl.
* Add eggs, vanilla, salt and baking soda and mix until combined.
* Mix in flour until the dough no longer sticks to the bowl, then fold in chocolate chips.
* Spoon out ~1.5tbsp balls onto baking sheet, and bake for 8-10 minutes, until slightly brown but still gooey.

## notes  
* Be careful not to overcook. The cookies can end up being hard and crunchy quite easily.
